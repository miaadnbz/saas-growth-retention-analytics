# Paid Social Activation Opportunity

## Business question

What could the business gain if Paid Social trials activated at a higher rate?

## Scenario

- Paid Social trial signups: 2,098
- Current 14-day activation rate: approximately 39%
- Scenario activation rate: approximately 49%
- Assumed improvement: 10 percentage points

## Estimated impact

- Approximately 210 additional activated trials
- Approximately 93 additional paid accounts
- Approximately 81 additional 90-day retained accounts
- Approximately $96,000 in annualized recurring revenue

## Interpretation

This is a planning scenario derived from observed funnel relationships. It is not a causal forecast. Customers who activate may already differ from customers who do not activate.

The proposed onboarding experiment is intended to estimate the incremental impact of the intervention.
